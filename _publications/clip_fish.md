**_Abstract_**: Inspired by snapping fish fins, this project explores an insect-scale swimmer that couples electromagnetic triggering with a bistable tail to achieve rapid, high-curvature strokes. Our contribution is a principle-driven design–model–test loop: compact actuation harnesses structural bistability for on–off energy release, while Discrete Elastic Rod (DER) modeling informs geometry and stiffness choices that shape motion quality. We emphasize transferable ideas rather than build parameters, showing how bistability, magnetic triggering, and elastic-rod reasoning together yield agile, robust behavior at cm scale. The result is a simple, manufacturable concept that preserves rich, controllable kinematics without exposing implementation specifics.
