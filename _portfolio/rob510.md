**_Abstract_**:
**Rigid-body geometry and screw theory** (rotation/transformations, exponential coordinates, twists/wrenches), **robot kinematics** (PoE & D-H, forward/inverse kinematics, Jacobian), and **dynamics** via Euler–Lagrange with properties of manipulator equations—followed by **multivariable nonlinear control** (Lyapunov stability, PD/feedback linearization, inverse dynamics, adaptive & passivity-based, force/impedance control). Students will model and simulate manipulators/mobile robots and design nonlinear controllers using **MATLAB/Mathematica**.
