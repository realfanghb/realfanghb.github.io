**_Abstract_**:
EECS 473 is a **capstone-style course** on designing **modern embedded systems—covering RTOS concepts, device drivers, PCB design with power integrity/EMI, RF/wireless communication, low-power techniques, DC/DC converter design, and rapid prototyping—culminating** in teams building a complete embedded system.
