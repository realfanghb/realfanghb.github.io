**_Abstract_**:
An application-driven introduction to feedback control for robotic systems, covering **classical methods—linearization, time/frequency response, Routh–Hurwitz, transfer functions, root locus, Bode/gain/phase margins, lead/lag/PID—and state-space techniques for multivariable systems (state transition matrix, LQR/LQI, observers/Kalman)**. Hands-on work in **MATLAB/Simulink** ties modeling, analysis, and controller design to robotics applications.
