**_Abstract_**:
This course covers the fundamentals of **continuous- and discrete-time signals, LTI system modeling via impulse response and convolution, frequency/transfer response, Fourier series/transform, sampling, modulation, filter design, and Laplace analysis with poles and zeros**. By the end, students will be able to analyze and design systems in both time and frequency domains and be prepared for follow-on control courses.
